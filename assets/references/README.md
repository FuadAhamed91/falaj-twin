# Facility references

Public reference material used to **place** and **model** the three real facilities.
Machine-readable data lives in [`../../shared/facilities.json`](../../shared/facilities.json).

**Licensing policy:** this repository records **source URLs and attributions only**.
No third-party photographs are redistributed here. Reference images are used to guide
modelling where their terms permit; final project assets are procedurally generated.

**Scope:** FALAJ has **no** access to these facilities' systems. Coordinates and
exteriors are real/public; all cyber assets are synthetic.

---

## 💧 Taweelah RO Desalination IWP (hero)
- **Location:** Al Taweelah, Abu Dhabi Emirate, UAE
- **Coordinates:** 24.7589, 54.6861 — *site-level (~300 m)*, Al Taweelah industrial coast
- **Official source:** <https://acwapower.com/en/what-we-do/projects/taweelah-ro-desalination-iwp/>
- **Public facts:** among the world's largest RO desalination plants by capacity; ACWA Power-led
  consortium; supplies the Abu Dhabi potable-water grid.
- **Visual reference notes (exterior):** large rectangular process halls parallel to the coast;
  rows of membrane-rack / pipe corridors; multiple cylindrical storage reservoirs; pretreatment
  basins toward the seawater intake; high-pressure pump houses; muted steel/concrete palette on a
  flat coastal desert site.
- **Modelling brief:** RO process hall with vaulted roof; 3 domed storage tanks; pump station;
  pipe corridors; pretreatment basins; illustrative PV detail. *Unknown / illustrative:* exact
  building count, internal layout, equipment placement.

## ❤️ Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi
- **Location:** Al Maryah Island, Abu Dhabi, UAE
- **Coordinates:** 24.5008, 54.3892 — *site-level (~150 m)*, Al Maryah Island
- **Official source:** <https://www.clevelandclinicabudhabi.ae/en> · architecture reference:
  <https://www.aedas.com/en/what-we-do/landscape/all_landscape/cleveland-clinic>
- **Public facts:** multispecialty hospital in the Cleveland Clinic network; distinctive
  clustered-tower silhouette on the Al Maryah global-market island.
- **Visual reference notes (exterior):** a podium base carrying a cluster of towers of varying
  heights with vertical glazed strips and landscaped sky-garden crowns; waterfront urban setting.
- **Modelling brief:** podium + 5 clustered towers of varying height; glazed south faces; planted
  crowns. *Unknown / illustrative:* precise tower count/heights, facade detail, internals.

## ⚡ DEWA Nad Hessa 132/11 kV Digital Substation
- **Location:** Nad Hessa, Dubai, UAE
- **Coordinates:** 25.2456, 55.3892 — *district-level (~1–2 km)*; exact substation parcel
  **not** publicly confirmed, so treated as representative.
- **Official source:** <https://dewa.gov.ae/en/about-us/media-publications/latest-news/2025/05/he-saeed-mohammed-al-tayer-inspects>
- **Public facts:** a DEWA 132/11 kV digital (IEC 61850) substation serving Nad Hessa; digital
  substations use fibre-based protection/control.
- **Visual reference notes (exterior):** GIS control building; outdoor transformers with porcelain
  bushings and radiator banks; steel switchyard gantries carrying busbars/insulators; fenced site.
- **Modelling brief:** GIS building; 3 transformers with bushings + radiators; switchyard gantries.
  *Unknown / illustrative:* exact parcel, equipment arrangement, bay count (representative only).

## Map geometry
Natural Earth 1:10m Cultural Vectors (**public domain**) via `world-atlas`, clipped to the UAE
region and projected with a latitude-corrected equirectangular projection.

## Coordinate verification
Coordinates are recorded at honest precision (site-level vs district-level) and cross-checked
against public map/encyclopedic sources. Where a precise parcel is not public (DEWA Nad Hessa),
the marker is explicitly district-level rather than a fabricated precise point.
