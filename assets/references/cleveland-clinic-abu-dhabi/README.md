## Summary

CCAD is a multispecialty hospital, about 99.6 m and 24 floors tall (CTBUH). It opened in 2015 on the **west waterfront of Al Maryah Island**, facing the channel towards Abu Dhabi island. HDR Architecture was the design architect, chosen through an international competition, and Aedas was architect of record.

Its silhouette is one of the most recognisable in Abu Dhabi, a **stack of rectangular boxes**:

- a long, low glass podium along the waterfront promenade;
- a gold/"onyx"-patterned block;
- two **mid-level horizontal glass bars** running E–W in opposite directions, the north one cantilevering towards the water with a **rooftop helipad**;
- on top of the bars, a long **N–S glass inpatient slab** of about 13 storeys, which appears to float above a **dark recessed transfer band**.

The tower is wrapped in **diamond-patterned, double-skin teal-green glazing**, which glows at night. The campus covers about 23 acres, with green roofs, courtyards and a landscaped lawn sloping to the promenade. A newer cancer centre (Fatima bint Mubarak Center, 2023) stands at its south end.

## Visual reference notes

- **Stacked-box composition.** This is the key to recognition. The building is a pile of separate rectangular volumes, each with its own cladding, as HDR describes it. Nothing is curved except the waterfront edge.
- **Inpatient slab, the top box.** A long N–S slab of about 128 × 41 m in plan and about 13 storeys, sitting high above the rest. It is clad in a **diamond lattice** of light mullions over teal-green glass, and the outer glass skin stands about 1.5 m off the inner wall. The **long face looks west over the water**. Near one end of the slab there is a narrow, darker vertical slot carrying a column of lit windows. The CCAD logo (Arabic and English) sits near an upper corner of the waterfront face; do not reproduce it.
- **Recessed transfer band.** A dark, set-back storey separates the slab from the bars, so the slab appears to hover.
- **Two horizontal glass bars at mid-height**, about 4 storeys each, also diamond-glazed and running E–W. The **north bar** (about 142 × 35 m) extends **west towards the waterfront** and cantilevers over the gold block. A **helipad** (green pad with a white "H" marking) sits on its roof. The **south bar** (about 119 × 40 m) extends **east**. The slab rests across the inner ends of both bars, giving a pinwheel/Z shape in plan.
- **Gold/"onyx" block**, about 125 × 50 m, N–S, under the north bar. Warm sand-gold patterned cladding with punched windows reads gold by day and copper-brown at dusk. It has a **green roof garden** on its exposed southern part.
- **Waterfront podium.** A long, low, fully glazed curtain-wall building of about 264 × 25 m and 4–5 storeys, with horizontal mullions. It runs along the curved promenade with **palms** in front. A lower glass pavilion of about 2 storeys with a grass roof sits to its north-west.
- **Central base.** A square block of about 117 × 114 m and about 5–7 storeys, clad in grey metal, with a **landscaped courtyard** that contains a rectangular water/glass feature.
- **Entrance side (north).** A glass entrance cube, a forecourt with a curving drop-off, and tree-lined plazas. A broad **lawn terrace slopes down to the promenade** on the west.
- **East side.** A linear park lined with dense trees, and a wide lawn south-east of the slab.
- **South end.** The Fatima bint Mubarak Center, about 78 × 77 m in plan. Its height has not been confirmed; the topping-off news mentions a tenth floor.
- **At night**, HDR's photos show the diamond pattern lit in **violet-blue**, with warm light in the gold block and a bright podium. All of this reflects in the channel.

**Materials.**

- Slab: a two-layer glass shader, i.e. an outer diamond-lattice mullion skin over a reflective inner curtain wall.
- Bars: the same diamond glazing, a little greener.
- Gold block: matte patterned panels with punched windows.
- Podium: clear-to-teal curtain wall with horizontal mullions.
- Base: grey metal panels.
- Roofs: green roof gardens, white/grey flat roofs, and a helipad decal on the north bar. The decal should be generic, with no logo.

**Surroundings.**

- West: the Al Maryah waterfront promenade with palms, then a turquoise channel about 150 m wide. Across it is Abu Dhabi island (Al Zahiyah/Tourist Club area) with a beach and hotels.
- North: Sowwah Square / ADGM, a cluster of dark-glass office towers plus hotel towers, then The Galleria mall. This is general geography; the tower count was not separately sourced. Dark-glass towers do appear to the left of CCAD in the CC BY photo.
- Road bridges cross the channel just north and south of the campus.
- East: a tree-lined park and an arterial road, with the mid- and high-rise Al Maryah business district beyond.
- North-east: Al Reem Island across a second channel. This is general geography and was not separately sourced.
